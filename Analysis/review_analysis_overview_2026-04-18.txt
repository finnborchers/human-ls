5) Preliminary Conclusions for HUMAN-LS
- The most robust barrier signals in this data are system-level:
  - access delays (waiting),
  - communication/respect deficits,
  - organizational/process breakdowns.
- Migrant-relevant issues are present and substantively important, especially language access and perceived discrimination, but are lower-frequency in explicit self-identification form.
- For next quantitative step, keep separate labels for:
  1) language-access barriers,
  2) discrimination against migrants,
  3) xenophobic complaints about foreign staff (separate to avoid bias in migrant-barrier estimates).

6) Method Note / Caveats
- This is a rapid keyword-guided qualitative scan, not a validated causal inference.
- A single review can hit multiple themes.
- Google reviews are self-selected and may overrepresent extreme experiences.
- Some migration/discrimination terms are ambiguous and need manual verification in later coding.
